What Patentforest does

From idea input to analysis and report, in one flow

01

Break your idea into elements

Enter your idea and its elements are extracted automatically.

02

Search prior art

Searches at the element level. 80% of ISR-cited documents appear in the top 50 results (internal study). Shows where your technology sits on a patent map.

03

Overlap check

Compares each element with prior art and scores the overlap, taking into account physical and scientific necessity and conceptual inclusion.

04

Leap check

Scores the distance from existing technology as a leap score, based on combinations of documents. Together with the overlap score, results are organized into X / Y / A.

05

Technical search report

Elements, analysis and prior art in 3 columns, with the relevant passages highlighted in color. Quick to review, and exportable to PDF.

06

Use the results in the next step

Feed results back to inventors or claim-drafting AI to help reduce overlap and increase the leap. (Patentforest does not draft claims or responses.)
