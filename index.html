Services

Three services, three starting points

Start from an idea or draft claims, a goal or a product. Wherever you start, the result goes to your patent attorney.

V
Patentforest VerifyIdea validation

Enter an idea or draft claims, get everything from prior-patent search to examination simulation

Your idea or draft claims are broken into elements, then Verify searches prior patents, places them on a patent map, and scores overlap and leap in one run. Results are organized in the same X / Y / A categories as an International Search Report (ISR).

Input
An idea (text) or draft claims
Output
Technical search report (PDF)
  • Element breakdownElements are extracted from your idea or draft claims automatically.
  • Prior-patent searchSearches at the element level. 80% of ISR-cited documents appear in the top 50 results (internal study).
  • MappingShows where your technology sits on a patent map.
  • Overlap checkCompares each element with prior art and scores the overlap, taking into account physical and scientific necessity and conceptual inclusion.
  • Leap checkScores the distance from existing technology based on combinations of documents. Together with overlap, results are organized into X / Y / A.
  • Technical search reportElements, analysis and prior art in 3 columns, with relevant passages highlighted in color. Export to PDF.
  • Next stepsFeed results back to inventors or claim-drafting AI to help reduce overlap and increase the leap. (Patentforest does not draft claims or responses.)

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can Patentforest judge novelty and inventive step, or give a patent opinion?
No. Patentforest does not judge novelty or inventive step, and does not give patent opinions. Use the overlap score as input when considering novelty, and the leap score when considering inventive step. For a judgment or opinion, please consult a patent attorney.
What is the examination simulation?
Each element of your idea or draft claims is compared with prior art, overlap and leap are scored, and results are organized in the same X / Y / A categories as an International Search Report (ISR). It does not guarantee the outcome of an actual examination.
