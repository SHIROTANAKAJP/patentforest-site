What Patentforest does

From claim input to analysis and report, in one flow

01

Break down claims

Enter a claim and its elements are extracted automatically and labeled with shared IDs.

02

Search prior art

Builds search queries from the elements and searches automatically. Results are ranked by match; add documents by number if needed.

03

Analyze disclosure, element by element

Checks each element against explicit text, inherent disclosure and conceptual inclusion, and classifies each claim as X / Y / A.

04

Find combinations

Identifies undisclosed elements, looks for them in other documents and for hints to combine, and names primary and secondary references.

05

Report with evidence

Claim elements, analysis and prior art in 3 columns, color-coded by element, with paragraph numbers. Export to PDF.

06

Use the results in the next step

Feed results back to inventors or claim-drafting AI to shape new claims, or find supporting passages when responding to rejections. Patentforest does not draft claims or responses.

07

Patent maps and competitor patents Planned for 2027

Detailed maps from element decomposition, and element-by-element comparison of third-party patents related to your products.
