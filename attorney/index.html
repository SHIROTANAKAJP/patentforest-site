Use

Three services and how a firm can use them

Each service prepares the material for a decision. The decision is made by the patent attorney.

V
VerifyValidation

Examination simulation

Extracts elements from claims or a specification, searches prior art, and evaluates the overlap for each element and the leap required to combine documents. Results are organized in the same X / Y / A categories as an ISR.

Use in a firmPrior-art search before filing, preparing responses to office actions, explaining results to clients.

S
SeedIdeation

Taking part from the R&D stage

Starting from what the client wants to achieve, Seed looks for ideas in a different direction from improving existing products (performance, cost) by combining elements of patents from five different fields. A selected idea can go straight into the examination simulation.

Use in a firmMeetings with the client's R&D team, invention harvesting, technical review before filing.

C
ClearPre-clearance

Third-party patent search from a product spec

Breaks a product spec into elements and lists similar third-party patents in X / A categories.

Use in a firmPreliminary search before a product launch, as a step before a full clearance search.

Practice

How it fits into your practice

Verify to cut time on search and examination work, Seed for consulting with clients, Clear for preliminary searches before a product launch.

V
VerifyLess time on search and examination work

Before

  • High search and drafting costsBuilding suitable search queries, reading documents in Japanese, English, European languages, Chinese, Korean, German, French and others, and preparing claim charts all take a great deal of time.
  • Over capacity in busy seasonsFilings and prosecution work pile up around clients' fiscal year-ends. Existing staff cannot handle it all, and new or urgent requests are sometimes turned down.
  • Barriers to taking on unfamiliar fields and foreign casesFor unfamiliar technical fields, or PCT and foreign applications that require close review of foreign documents, search costs do not pay off and firms hesitate to take the work.
→

After

  • Automation and time savingsElement extraction, mapping, claim charts and X / Y / A categories are generated automatically. Paragraphs showing matches and differences are highlighted, and everything can be downloaded in one click.
  • Substantive evaluation based on the ISPEEvaluation follows the PCT International Search and Preliminary Examination Guidelines (ISPE). Before any country's local rules, a common logic checks the essential question: do X or Y documents that deny patentability exist in the world's patent publications?
  • Handling more filings and early-stage ideasRun an examination simulation quickly on the growing number of AI-assisted filings and on immature early-stage ideas. Concrete improvement points can go back to the client right away, which helps turn a consultation into an engagement.
  • Taking on unfamiliar and adjacent fieldsAutomatic element breakdown and novelty / inventive-step analysis give a quick view of the landscape and the points of difference, even in unfamiliar fields. Work in adjacent fields that was turned down for lack of staff becomes possible.
  • Making junior engineers and paralegals productiveWork can proceed from the AI's first-pass analysis and highlights, so less experienced staff can produce searches and groundwork of a consistent standard.
  • Easier client explanations that clients acceptIP landscape and X / Y / A results show visually and logically why a given claim scope was chosen.
S
SeedConsulting for clients

Before

  • Involvement starts just before filingFew chances to take part in setting the direction of R&D.
  • Limited use of technical knowledgeFew occasions to apply a deep understanding of the technology to proposing new themes.
  • Competing on improvement inventionsClients' inventions are often limited to improvements such as better performance or lower cost.
→

After

  • Taking part from the R&D stageWork with the client from what they want to achieve, through theme setting and idea development, to filing.
  • Ideas in white spaceIdea proposals that combine elements of patents from five different fields. Useful for choosing themes away from areas crowded with competitors' improvement patents.
  • Linked to VerifyA selected idea goes straight into the examination simulation. Decide whether to file, and on claim scope, after checking the outlook for novelty and inventive step.
  • Offered as a consulting serviceInvention-harvesting meetings and technical theme proposals, offered as a service separate from filing work.
C
ClearPreliminary freedom-to-operate search

Before

  • The burden of freedom-to-operate searchesSearching, reading and comparing third-party patents for each element of a product. High effort and cost per case.
  • Searches skippedSome clients skip a search before product launch because the cost does not seem worth it.
  • Deciding the scope of the searchLittle material, before a full search, for judging which elements and which competitors need attention.
→

After

  • Third-party patent search at the element levelBreaks the product spec into elements and searches third-party patents for each element. Maps how the product corresponds to third-party patents.
  • A list of similar patents in X / A categoriesLists similar third-party patents and organizes them in X / A categories. Infringement is judged by the patent attorney.
  • Setting the scope of the full searchUse the preliminary results to narrow down the elements and competitors that need attention, then agree the scope and cost of the full search with the client.
  • Offered as a preliminary search serviceA preliminary search before product launch, offered as a lighter service than a full search.

Roles

What Patentforest does and what the patent attorney does

Patentforest does not judge or give opinions on patentability or infringement.

PATENTFOREST

Search and analysis

Element extraction, prior-art search, element-by-element comparison, overlap and leap scores, patent maps, X / Y / A categories and the report.

PATENT ATTORNEY

Judgment

Reviewing the prior art found, judging novelty, inventive step and infringement, and deciding the approach to filings, amendments and responses.

PATENT ATTORNEY

Client explanations

Explaining results to the client based on the report, and proposing next steps and further searches.
